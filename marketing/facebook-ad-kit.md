## 1. WHAT'S IMPORTANT IN THE PHILIPPINE SWINE INDUSTRY RIGHT NOW

| Reality (mid-2026) | Why it matters to a farm owner |
|---|---|
| **Feed = 57–70% of total production cost**, blended at ~₱39/kg and still rising ₱1–2/kg with no rollback in sight | The single biggest expense line on any farm — whoever controls feed controls profit [2](https://baboyph.com/blog/cost-to-raise-pig-philippines/), [3](https://baboyph.com/blog/philippine-feed-economics/), [5](https://baboyph.com/pigs/state-of-the-industry/) |
| **FCR is the hidden profit variable**: the gap between FCR 2.5 and 3.5 costs ≈ **₱3,145 per head** (~₱31k per 10-head batch) | Most backyard raisers have *no idea* of their real FCR — they fly blind on their biggest cost [3](https://baboyph.com/blog/philippine-feed-economics/) |
| **Prices are strong but margins are thin**: liveweight ₱183–218/kg (DA floor ₱210), piglets ₱4,100–4,300; a 10-head batch needs ~₱140k upfront and nets only ~₱29.5k after mortality | High prices = restocking boom; thin margins = only *managed* farms survive [1](https://m.efeedlink.com/contents/05-12-2026/6ce74bcf-1448-4fc1-9611-0a77fc2ba50c-0711.html), [2](https://baboyph.com/blog/cost-to-raise-pig-philippines/), [4](https://baboyph.com/blog/pig-farming-profit-10-pigs/) |
| **National herd still ~30% below pre-ASF (9.01M heads)**; ASF still active in scattered barangays | Restocking window is open — but biosecurity & vaccination discipline decide who lives to sell [5](https://baboyph.com/pigs/state-of-the-industry/) |
| **Industry average cost of production ≈ ₱180/kg vs farmgate ₱180–190/kg** in weaker markets | In a bad month, the unmanaged farm *loses money*. Data is the difference [4](https://baboyph.com/blog/pig-farming-profit-10-pigs/) |

**The emotional hook:** every farm owner knows feed money is leaking — spoiled sacks, wrong-stage feeding, unmeasured FCR, sows that eat but don't farrow, missed boosters. The industry numbers above make that leak *quantifiable in pesos*.
