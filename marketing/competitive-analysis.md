## The verdict — where you stand

**You are the only app that combines Tier-1 genetics + Tier-2 mobile field tools + commerce (reservations, semen sales, POS) at a flat small-holder price, built *for* the Philippines, offline-first, no install.**

A 50-sow backyard farm would pay PigCHAMP-style vendors **US$75–150/yr + setup and a desktop**; with you they pay **₱5,988/yr (~US$105) for the whole farm**, every feature, on the phone they already own — and the ₱499 tier undercuts every Western competitor by 5–10×.

### Your 5 winning cards (use these in ads)
1. **"Herdbook in your pocket"** — 4-generation pedigree + QR certificates no logbook app can touch.
2. **Offline-first, install-free** — works in a barn with no signal; competitors need store apps + sync accounts.
3. **Commerce built-in** — reservations with prepayments, semen reselling, POS: competitors *record*, you also *sell*.
4. **Filipino-first** — ₱, local breeds, Taglish-friendly, Ocampo-realistic workflows.
5. **Zero-risk trial** — 15 days free with one-tap subscribe + data migration; enterprise vendors quote by email for weeks.

### Your 3 honest gaps (roadmap, not emergencies)
1. **Benchmark network** — PigCHAMP's moat is benchmarking your herd against thousands [2](https://wifitalents.com/best/sow-management-software/). *Fix: anonymized PH regional benchmarks from your own growing user base (you already have KPI Center).*
2. **Vet compliance engine** — Data Pig wins vets with prescription sync + withdrawal periods [5](https://www.datapig.com.au/). *Fix: withdrawal-period auto-warnings on treatments (partial data already recorded).*
3. **IoT controllers** — Fancom/Stal tie into climate/feed hardware [2](https://wifitalents.com/best/sow-management-software/). *Low priority for PH smallholders; your RFID + BLE-scale modules already cover the practical hardware need.*
